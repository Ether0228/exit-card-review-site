补救模块

err-resume-purpose-interview

Students believe a resume should be a detailed account of their entire life and work history, rather than a concise summary designed to secure a job interview.

Explain: A resume is a short, highly structured summary of education, work history, and relevant life experiences, with the sole purpose of securing a job interview. It is not an autobiography or a job application form. The goal is to get an interview, not to get hired directly.

Example: Consider two resume summaries: (1) 'I was born in 2000, attended Springfield Elementary, and have always enjoyed reading. I worked at a grocery store for two years where I stocked shelves and helped customers.' (2) 'Detail-oriented high school graduate with 2 years of customer service experience at a busy grocery store, recognized for efficiency and positive customer feedback.' The second summary is targeted and designed to get an interview; the first is too detailed and unfocused.

err-resume-quantification

Students think listing duties without quantifiable results is sufficient for a resume.

Explain: Every experience bullet point on a resume must follow the action statement formula: [action verb] + [task description] + [execution method] + [quantifiable, verifiable result]. Employers want to see the impact of your work, not just a list of duties. Numbers make your achievements concrete and memorable.

Example: coordinated school fundraising events, leading a team of 10 volunteers, raising $2,000 for local charities.

err-resume-ai-generation

You think that using AI or translation software to generate part or all of your resume is okay, perhaps because you believe you can edit it or that resumes are not academic work. However, the course policy clearly states that any work generated by AI or translation tools will receive an automatic zero, with no exceptions.

Explain: The course has a strict plagiarism policy: any work fully or partially generated via translation tools or AI receives an automatic zero score. This applies to the resume assignment. Using AI or translation software is considered cheating because it prevents you from developing your own writing skills. Even if you edit the AI output, it is still not your original work. You must write everything yourself and have a peer review your draft. The purpose of the assignment is to practice real-world resume writing, and using AI undermines that learning.

Example: If you use an AI tool to write a bullet point like 'Managed a team of 5 volunteers, increasing event attendance by 20%,' and then submit it as your own, that is plagiarism. You would receive a zero on the assignment, and it could affect your course grade. Instead, you should write your own bullet points based on your actual experiences, using the action statement formula taught in class.

err-resume-personal-pronouns

Students think it is acceptable to use first-person pronouns in resumes, not understanding that professional resumes should avoid them to maintain conciseness and formality.

Explain: Resumes must avoid personal pronouns like 'I', 'me', 'my', or 'we' because the reader knows the resume is about you. Using pronouns makes the writing less concise and less professional. Instead, start each bullet with a strong action verb and focus on the task and result.

Example: Incorrect: 'I coordinated a team of volunteers for the school carnival.' Correct: 'Coordinated a team of 10 volunteers for the school carnival, increasing attendance by 25%.'
