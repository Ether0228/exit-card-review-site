Q6. You are writing a paragraph to help a friend decide between buying a mountain bike or a road bike. Which text structure would be most effective?

补救模块

err-confuse-compare-description

Students may think that describing multiple features of a single subject is the same as comparing two subjects. They focus on details without recognizing the need for multiple subjects and explicit comparison.

Explain: Description and Compare and Contrast are two different text structures. Description provides detailed information about one subject to create a vivid picture. Compare and Contrast examines two or more subjects to show their similarities and differences. The key difference: Description is about one subject; Compare and Contrast involves multiple subjects and uses words like 'both', 'however', 'unlike'.

Example: For example, a Description passage: 'The golden retriever has a thick, water-repellent coat, a friendly face, and a strong, muscular build.' This describes only one breed. A Compare and Contrast passage: 'Golden retrievers are friendly, while German shepherds are protective. Both are intelligent.' This compares two breeds.
