Q1. Factor 27x³ + 125 completely. Which of the following is the correct factorization?

Official: C3 | Node: MHF4U-C-OE03 | Source: https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/math1112currb.pdf

Learning Goal: lg-1

Misconception: Student may think the middle term is +ab (for sum) or misplace signs.

Remediation → err-sum-cubes-sign | Retest → err-sum-cubes-sign

  1. ✓ A. (3x + 5)(9x² - 15x + 25)
  2. B. (3x + 5)(9x² + 15x + 25)
    Sum of cubes formula has a minus sign in the middle term: (a+b)(a² - ab + b²). You chose +ab.
  3. C. (3x - 5)(9x² + 15x + 25)
    This looks like difference of cubes, but the original expression is a sum. Also check the sign of the binomial.
  4. D. (3x + 5)(9x² - 30x + 25)
    The constant term 25 is correct, but the middle term should be -ab = -15x, not -30x.

补救模块

err-sum-cubes-sign

The student confuses the sign of the middle term in the sum of cubes formula. For a³ + b³, the correct trinomial is a² - ab + b² (minus ab), but the student may think it is a² + ab + b² (plus ab).

Explain: The sum of cubes formula is a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² - ab + b²). Notice the middle term is -ab (minus a times b). This is opposite to what you might expect from expanding (a+b)(a²+ab+b²) which would give a³+2a²b+2ab²+b³, not a³+b³. The correct factorization comes from the identity a³+b³ = (a+b)(a² - ab + b²). Remember: 'Same sign, opposite sign, always plus' - the binomial has the same sign as the sum, the trinomial has the opposite sign for the middle term, and the last term is always positive.

Example: Factor 27x³ + 125. Here a=3x, b=5. Apply formula: (3x+5)((3x)² - (3x)(5) + 5²) = (3x+5)(9x² - 15x + 25).

err-synthetic-division-coefficients

Students often omit zero coefficients for missing terms when setting up synthetic division, leading to incorrect results.

Explain: Synthetic division requires a row of coefficients from the highest degree term down to the constant. If a term is missing (e.g., no x² term), you must include a 0 as its coefficient to maintain the correct order. Forgetting a zero shifts all subsequent values and produces a wrong quotient.

Example: Divide x³ - 2x + 1 by (x - 3). Write the polynomial in standard form: x³ + 0x² - 2x + 1. So the coefficients are 1, 0, -2, 1. With c = 3, synthetic division: bring down 1; multiply 1*3=3, add to 0 gives 3; multiply 3*3=9, add to -2 gives 7; multiply 7*3=21, add to 1 gives 22. The quotient is x² + 3x + 7 with remainder 22.

err-factoring-incomplete

Students may stop after one application of difference of cubes without checking if the binomial factor itself is a difference of cubes that can be factored further.

Explain: Some polynomial expressions, like x⁹ - 512, require applying the difference of cubes formula more than once. After factoring as (x³ - 8)(x⁶ + 8x³ + 64), the factor x³ - 8 is itself a difference of cubes (since 8 = 2³). Always check if any factor can be factored further using the same or other methods.

Example: Factor x⁹ - 512. Step 1: Rewrite as (x³)³ - 8³ and apply difference of cubes: (x³ - 8)(x⁶ + 8x³ + 64). Step 2: Factor x³ - 8 = (x - 2)(x² + 2x + 4). Final: (x - 2)(x² + 2x + 4)(x⁶ + 8x³ + 64).

err-quartic-substitution-neglect

Students may not realize that a quartic expression can be rewritten as a quadratic by substituting y = x², making it easier to factor.

Explain: Many quartic expressions can be written as quadratics in disguise. For example, x⁴ + 4x² - 5 looks like a quartic, but notice the exponents: 4 and 2. This matches the pattern of a quadratic if we let y = x². Then x⁴ becomes y². So we get y² + 4y - 5, which factors into (y + 5)(y - 1). Substituting back gives (x² + 5)(x² - 1). Finally, factor the difference of squares: (x² + 5)(x - 1)(x + 1).

Example: Factor x⁴ + 4x² - 5. Step 1: Let y = x². Then x⁴ = y². Step 2: Factor y² + 4y - 5 = (y + 5)(y - 1). Step 3: Replace y with x²: (x² + 5)(x² - 1). Step 4: Factor x² - 1 as (x - 1)(x + 1). Final answer: (x² + 5)(x - 1)(x + 1).

err-simple-trinomial-decomposition

The student believes that decomposition is always required for factoring trinomials, not realizing that when the leading coefficient is 1, the factoring is straightforward by finding two numbers that multiply to the constant term and add to the linear coefficient.

Explain: When factoring a simple trinomial of the form x² + bx + c, you do NOT need to use decomposition. Decomposition is for trinomials with a leading coefficient other than 1. For simple trinomials, the factoring is straightforward: find two numbers that multiply to c and add to b. Then the factors are (x + p)(x + q) where p and q are those numbers.

Example: Factor x² + 7x + 12. Find two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7: 3 and 4. So x² + 7x + 12 = (x + 3)(x + 4).
