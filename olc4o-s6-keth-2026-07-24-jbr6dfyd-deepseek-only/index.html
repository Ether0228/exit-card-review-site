补救模块

err-olc4o-assignment-core-logic

The student thinks the assignment is about gathering general opinions or creating a photo gallery, rather than collecting unique personal stories from school community members.

Explain: The 'Humans of My High School' assignment is inspired by the 'Humans of New York' project, which has over 10 million Instagram followers. Its core is to collect unique, individual stories from school community members, not general opinions or philosophies. The goal is to document personal narratives that reveal the diversity and humanity within the school. Photos may accompany stories, but the focus is on the story, not the photo.

Example: Model assignment from student Chen Xuanlan demonstrates natural expression and focuses on a single person's unique experience, not broad opinions. In contrast, Zhu Yucheng's assignment was flagged for AI-generated content because it used overly polished photos, rare words like 'weekway', and unrealistic timelines—all of which detract from authentic storytelling.

err-olc4o-interview-ethics

Students may think that recording every word verbatim ensures accuracy, or that leading questions are acceptable to guide the story.

Explain: Interview ethics require respecting the interviewee, demonstrating active listening, not forcing answers, and maintaining confidentiality as requested. At the start, thank the interviewee, explain the purpose, and state they can skip questions. During the interview, only record keywords and maintain eye contact. At the closing, thank them again, confirm which parts are not to be shared, and inform them about the publication channel and timeline.

Example: Standard opening: 'Thank you for allowing me to interview you today. My name is _______. Can you please tell me your name?' This shows respect and sets a comfortable tone.
