    补救模块

    err-unit1-test-requirements-misunderstanding

    Students may think the essay only requires describing one policy or summarizing the case, and may omit cross-comparison, resource allocation analysis, or identification of multiple unemployment types.

    Explain: The Unit 1 test essay on 'The Economic Crisis in Rivermark' must be a structured analytical essay. You must cross-compare at least two different policy approaches, analyze how resources are allocated under each, identify two or more types of unemployment present in the case, and support your analysis with evidence from the case study and our in-class discussions. The essay should be about 700 words (3 double-spaced pages).

    Example: For example, you could compare a market-based policy (like deregulation) with a government intervention policy (like stimulus spending). Explain how each allocates scarce resources (e.g., tax incentives vs. direct spending) and identify unemployment types such as structural (due to industry decline) and cyclical (due to recession). Use details from the Rivermark case, like job losses in manufacturing and the housing bubble.
