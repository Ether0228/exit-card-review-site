Q1. Which method of heat transfer requires particles to be in direct contact?

errorId: err-heat-transfer-medium | Bloom: understand | Scaffold: L1

Achievement: Knowledge and Understanding | Action: identify | OSSD: Demonstrate an understanding of work, efficiency, power, gravitational potential

Official: D3 | Node: SPH3U-D-OE03 | Source: https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/2009science11_12.pdf

Learning Goal: lg-1

Misconception: Believing radiation needs a medium, or that conduction does not involve direct particle interaction.

Remediation → err-heat-transfer-medium | Retest → err-heat-transfer-medium

  1. ✓ A. Conduction
    Correct. Conduction occurs through particle collisions when objects are in direct contact.
  2. B. Convection
    Convection involves fluid movement, not direct particle-to-particle contact at the macroscopic level. While it requires a medium, the defining feature is bulk fluid flow.
  3. C. Radiation
    Radiation transfers heat via electromagnetic waves and does not require any medium; it can travel through vacuum.
  4. D. All of them require direct contact
    Only conduction requires direct contact between particles. Radiation does not even require a medium.

Q2. A satellite in space receives heat from the Sun. Which method of heat transfer is primarily responsible?

errorId: err-heat-transfer-medium | Bloom: apply | Scaffold: L2

Achievement: Application | Action: apply | OSSD: Demonstrate an understanding of work, efficiency, power, gravitational potential

Official: D3 | Node: SPH3U-D-OE03 | Source: https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/2009science11_12.pdf

Learning Goal: lg-1

Misconception: Thinking that heat cannot travel through vacuum, or that convection or conduction could occur in space.

Remediation → err-heat-transfer-medium | Retest → err-heat-transfer-medium

  1. A. Conduction
    Conduction requires direct contact between objects; space is a vacuum with no particles for contact.
  2. B. Convection
    Convection requires fluid movement; there is no fluid (liquid or gas) in the vacuum of space.
  3. ✓ C. Radiation
    Correct. Radiation transfers heat via electromagnetic waves and does not require a medium, so it works in space.
  4. D. Both convection and radiation
    Convection cannot occur in space because there is no fluid. Only radiation can transfer heat in a vacuum.

Q3. Describe how a hot air balloon rises, identify the heat transfer method involved, and explain why this method cannot occur in a solid.

errorId: err-heat-transfer-medium | Bloom: evaluate | Scaffold: L3

Achievement: Communication | Action: justify | OSSD: Demonstrate an understanding of work, efficiency, power, gravitational potential

Official: D3 | Node: SPH3U-D-OE03 | Source: https://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/curriculum/secondary/2009science11_12.pdf

Learning Goal: lg-2

Misconception: Confusing convection with radiation, or thinking solids can convect heat.

Remediation → err-heat-transfer-medium | Retest → err-heat-transfer-medium

    Rubric: Full marks require: identifying convection as the method, describing that hot air expands and rises due to lower density, and explaining that convection requires fluid movement (particles can move freely), whereas in a solid, particles are fixed and cannot flow.

    Sample: A hot air balloon rises because the burner heats the air inside, causing it to expand and become less dense than the surrounding cold air. This less dense hot air rises, carrying the balloon upward. This is convection. Convection cannot happen in a solid because the particles in a solid are held in fixed positions and cannot flow like liquids or gases.

    补救模块

    err-heat-transfer-medium

    Students may think radiation requires a medium to travel through, or that conduction and convection do not involve particle interactions. In fact, conduction uses direct contact between particles, convection uses the bulk movement of fluids, and radiation transfers energy via electromagnetic photons without any medium.

    Explain: Heat transfers in three ways. Conduction transfers heat through direct contact, like a hot coffee cup heating a hand. Convection transfers heat through fluid flow, like hot water rising and cold water sinking during boiling. Radiation transfers heat as electromagnetic photons without requiring a medium, like solar heat reaching Earth.

    Example: A satellite in space receives heat from the Sun. Space is a vacuum, so there is no medium for conduction or convection. Only radiation works because it does not require a medium.
