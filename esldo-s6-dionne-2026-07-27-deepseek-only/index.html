    补救模块

    err-eros-psyche-name-confusion

    The student does not distinguish between Greek and Roman names for the same mythological figures, thinking they are separate or unfamiliar with the cross-references.

    Explain: In the myth of Eros and Psyche, the characters have both Greek and Roman names. For example, Eros is the Greek name for the god of love, and his Roman name is Cupid. Similarly, Aphrodite is the Greek goddess of love and beauty, and her Roman name is Venus. Zeus, the king of the gods, is called Jupiter in Roman. These are the same gods, just called different names in different cultures. It is important to remember both names so you can understand stories from both traditions.

    Example: When reading the myth, you may see 'Cupid' used instead of 'Eros'. They refer to the same character. Likewise, 'Venus' is the Roman name for 'Aphrodite'. Knowing these pairs helps avoid confusion.

    err-eros-psyche-task-order

    Students may believe the tasks can be completed in any order, or they may skip one task entirely, not realizing the sequence is fixed.

    Explain: Psyche was given three tasks by Aphrodite, and they must be done in a specific order. The first task was to sort a huge pile of mixed seeds. The second was to collect golden wool from dangerous wild sheep. The third was to fetch water from a steep cliff-top river. Remembering the order helps you follow the story.

    Example: In the myth, after Psyche sorted the seeds, she faced the challenge of collecting golden wool, and finally she had to fetch water from a cliff.

    err-eros-psyche-theme-overgeneralization

    Students believe the only theme in the myth is love, ignoring trust, curiosity, and forgiveness as equally valid themes.

    Explain: Myths often have multiple themes, not just one. In the myth of Eros and Psyche, love is a major theme, but trust, curiosity, and forgiveness are also important. Trust is shown when Psyche breaks her promise to Eros by looking at him. Curiosity leads to negative consequences when she opens the forbidden beauty box. Forgiveness is shown when Eros rescues Psyche and they reunite.

    Example: Psyche's curiosity caused her to open the box, leading to a deep sleep. This shows that unchecked curiosity can have negative consequences, a theme beyond love.
