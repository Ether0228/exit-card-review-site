补救模块

err-assignment-external-research

Students may think they can strengthen their analysis by including outside research, even if properly cited. They may also believe external research is allowed for certain sections like cause analysis.

Explain: The assignment instructions explicitly state that no external research beyond the provided Tesco case is allowed. All analysis and recommendations must be based solely on the Tesco case and the concepts learned in Unit 2 lessons (4,5,6). The purpose is to assess your ability to apply course knowledge to a given scenario without relying on external sources.

Example: For the cause analysis, you must identify causes directly from the case text, not from external articles about leadership. If you write 'According to a leadership article, ...' that would violate the rule.

err-assignment-problem-statement-length

Students may think that a longer, more detailed problem statement provides better analysis, but the assignment requires a concise 1-2 sentence statement.

Explain: The problem/opportunity statement in your report must be exactly 1-2 sentences. It should clearly state the main issue or opportunity in the Tesco case without going into causes or details. Keeping it brief forces you to focus on the core problem.

Example: For the Tesco case, a correct problem statement could be: 'Tesco's global expansion strategy has led to operational inefficiencies and cultural misalignment, resulting in declining profits in international markets.' (1 sentence) or 'Tesco faces declining profits due to rapid global expansion. The company must reassess its entry strategies.' (2 sentences).

err-assignment-citations-missing

Students often believe that when using only one source, citations are unnecessary because the source is obvious. They think the task is simply to present findings, not to cite them.

Explain: Even when using a single provided source, you must include in-text citations and a reference list. This is a requirement of academic integrity and proper formatting. Every time you reference an idea or data from the Tesco case, you must cite it. The reference list gives full details.

Example: For example, if you mention that 'Tesco faced declining sales in 2015,' you should put (Tesco Case, 2026) after the sentence, and in the reference list write: 'Tesco Case Study. (2026). [Provided by instructor].'

err-assignment-format-requirements

The student may think that an executive summary is a required section, or may not realize that the report must include a cover page, table of contents, in-text citations, and a reference list with specific formatting (12pt, double spacing).

Explain: For the Unit 2 Lesson 2 case study report, you must follow these formatting requirements: use 12pt font and double spacing throughout; include a cover page (with your name, course, etc.), a table of contents, in-text citations, and a reference list. The required sections are: fact summary (bullet points), problem/opportunity statement (1-2 sentences), cause analysis (bullet points), alternatives (2-3 with pros/cons table), and implementation plan (selected option and steps). An executive summary is NOT required.

Example: Your report should start with a cover page, then a table of contents. The body begins with the fact summary in bullet points. Each time you use information from the Tesco case, add an in-text citation (e.g., Tesco, 2026). At the end, include a reference list entry for the case.
