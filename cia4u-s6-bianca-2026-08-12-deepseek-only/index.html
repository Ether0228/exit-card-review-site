      补救模块

      err-test-missing-required-section

      The student thinks the written response only needs a general discussion of fiscal and monetary policy, not a specific structure with five required sections.

      Explain: The Unit 3 test is a written research paper. Your response must include five mandatory sections. Missing any one means the response does not meet the test requirements. The five mandatory sections are: Canadian fiscal policy definition, Canada's monetary targets, policy interaction, current policy assessment, and improvement recommendations. Each section must be clearly headed in your paper.

      Example: If a student writes about fiscal policy and monetary policy but forgets the 'policy interaction' section, they have four sections, not five. The response would be marked incomplete. The required list is: 1) Canadian fiscal policy definition, 2) Canada's monetary targets, 3) policy interaction, 4) current policy assessment, 5) improvement recommendations.

      err-test-insufficient-citations

      The student thinks one or two sources are enough to support an argument, or that listing a source is the same as properly citing it.

      Explain: The Unit 3 test requires all arguments to be supported by at least three properly cited reliable sources. Reliable sources include class notes, course materials, and credible economic research. A proper citation means you clearly identify the source for each claim, not just mention it in general. Meeting the minimum of three sources for each key argument ensures your paper is well-supported and meets the test expectation.

      Example: If you write: 'Canada's monetary policy uses inflation targeting to guide interest rates,' you must support that with at least three sources. For instance, you could cite the Bank of Canada website, your class notes from the lesson on monetary targets, and a credible economics textbook. Each source must be properly cited in the text and listed in your references.

      err-test-incorrect-format

      The student does not follow the required page length (2-3 pages), double-spacing, clear headings, or research paper style, possibly submitting a formal essay or using incorrect formatting.

      Explain: The Unit 3 test is a written research paper that must follow specific format guidelines: it must be 2-3 pages long, double-spaced, structured with clear headings, and presented as a research paper rather than a formal essay.

      Example: A correctly formatted response would be 2-3 pages long, double-spaced, with each of the five mandatory sections clearly headed (e.g., 'Canadian Fiscal Policy Definition', 'Canada's Monetary Targets', 'Policy Interaction', 'Current Policy Assessment', 'Improvement Recommendations').

      err-test-communication-violation

      The student may believe they can speak aloud or turn off their camera during the test, not realizing that microphones are prohibited, questions must be typed in chat, and continuous camera presence is required for attendance verification.

      Explain: During the Unit 3 test, all microphones must be off. If you have a question, send it through the meeting chat box. You must keep your camera on for the entire test so the instructor can verify your attendance. This rule helps maintain a quiet, secure testing environment and ensures everyone is present.

      Example: Imagine you are writing your test and you realize you are unsure about what to include in the policy interaction section. Instead of unmuting and asking aloud, you type your question in the meeting chat. Meanwhile, your camera remains on so the instructor sees you are still working.

      err-test-late-submission

      The student may think that the submission deadline is flexible or that a short delay will be accepted. They may not recognize that the 10-minute upload window is a strict requirement, and that any technical issue must be reported immediately by email to the instructor.

      Explain: For this Unit 3 test, the submission protocol is strict. Once the briefing ends, you have exactly 10 minutes to upload your completed test document. There is no grace period. You must also double-check that you are uploading the correct dedicated test document. If you experience any technical issue during submission, you must email the instructor immediately, not wait or keep silent. This ensures that the issue is documented and that you are following the official procedure.

      Example: Suppose the briefing ends at 2:00 PM. You must have your test document uploaded by 2:10 PM. If your internet connection fails at 2:08 PM, you should immediately email the instructor to report the problem and keep trying to upload. You should not assume that a late upload after 2:10 PM will be accepted without explanation.
